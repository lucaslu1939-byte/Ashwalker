# Before we begin

Ashwalker is a wellness companion, not a medical provider. Please read this before we start.

**This is general wellness support, not medical advice.** The suggestions you'll hear here — around nutrition, lifestyle, and emotional wellbeing — are general in nature. They are not a diagnosis, a treatment plan, or a substitute for care from a licensed doctor, dietitian, or therapist.

**For anything specific to your health, please see a professional.** If you're dealing with a diagnosed condition, concerning symptoms, or anything that feels urgent, this app will encourage you to bring it to a qualified professional rather than try to resolve it here.

**Your information stays on your device.** What you share here — your goals, your answers, your conversation — is stored locally on your phone. It isn't sent anywhere except to generate the coach's replies in the moment.

**You're always in control.** You can stop, skip a question, or come back later. Nothing here is a commitment beyond what feels right for you.

By continuing, you understand this is general wellness support, not medical advice, and that you'll seek a licensed professional for anything diagnostic or symptom-specific.
